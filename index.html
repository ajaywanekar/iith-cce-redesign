About the programme

Every organisation pays a Friction Tax.

Individually, these moments feel like the cost of doing business. Added up across people and time, they become a measurable business cost. It is almost never on the boardroom agenda, because nobody has put a number on it.

This programme does exactly that. Over two days at IIT Hyderabad, you identify the structural friction in your own organisation and calculate its annual cost with the Friction Cost Formula. Then you design a tested intervention that links employee experience to the bottom line.

You leave with

A completed diagnosis · a costed Friction Tax figure · evidence-based redesign ideas · a 30-day pilot plan ready to run.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is accommodation included in the fee?

Yes. The ₹38,000 (+ GST) fee includes your stay on the IIT Hyderabad campus and the meals listed in the schedule.

Is this programme online or in person?

It is an in-person, residential retreat held at DSSI Studios, Department of Design, IIT Hyderabad.

Will I receive a certificate?

Yes. Every participant who completes the programme receives a certificate of completion from IIT Hyderabad.

Can faculty members or students attend?

Yes. Faculty and postgraduate students are welcome, along with leaders, HR/OD/L&D professionals, consultants and corporate real estate leaders.

Do I need a design background?

No. The programme is built on learning by doing, and every tool is introduced and practised in the room.

What is the cancellation or refund policy?

Please contact the programme team at +91 91568 52698 before registering for the current refund and transfer policy.
